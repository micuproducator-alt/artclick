Artcllick - Batch Product Photos

A Photoshop plugin that removes backgrounds and creates ready-to-use listing images for a whole folder of product photos in one pass, with optional export presets for Instagram square, Instagram portrait, and an Amazon-compliant pure white background version.

1Description of the Plugin

The Plugin lets you select a folder of raw product photos and an export folder, choose a brand preset or custom background color, canvas size and padding, and process the entire folder in one click. Each photo has its background removed, is placed on the chosen canvas with an optional soft shadow, and is exported in the format and sizes you selected.

Background removal is performed by a third-party AI provider that you connect yourself using your own API key (currently remove.bg or Clipdrop, both of which offer a free usage tier). The Plugin does not include or resell access to any background-removal service; you are responsible for creating and managing your own account and API key with the provider of your choice.
