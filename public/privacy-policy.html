Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: July 29, 2026✍ Artcllick

This Privacy Policy explains how Artcllick ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Artcllick - Batch Product Photos plugin for Adobe Photoshop ("the Plugin"). In short: the Plugin does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data or photos on our servers.

The Plugin runs locally inside Photoshop. It does not have its own backend or database, does not create user accounts, and does not send your photos, files, or personal information to Artcllick at any point.

1Data the Plugin Does Not Collect

The Plugin does not collect, log, or store:

  • Your product photos or any images you process;
  • Your name, email address, or other personal identifiers;
  • Your API keys for remove.bg or Clipdrop;
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking data;
  • Payment information (purchases are handled entirely by Adobe Marketplace).

2How Your Photos Are Actually Processed

When you run the Plugin on a folder of photos, each image is sent directly from your own computer to the third-party AI provider you have chosen to connect (remove.bg or Clipdrop), using the API key you obtained and entered yourself. Processed images are returned directly to your computer and saved to the export folder you selected. Artcllick is never an intermediary in this exchange and has no visibility into, or copy of, the images being processed.

Because this exchange happens between your computer and the provider you chose, that provider's own privacy policy governs how your images and API key are handled on their end:

  • remove.bg Privacy Policy
  • Clipdrop Privacy Policy

3Where Your API Key Is Stored

Your remove.bg or Clipdrop API key is stored locally on your own computer, within the Plugin's local settings inside Photoshop. It is never transmitted to Artcllick or to any server we operate, and is never shared with anyone other than the provider you selected, for the sole purpose of authenticating your background-removal requests.

4Table: Data Flow at a Glance

Data Where it goes Does Artcllick see it?
Your product photos Your computer → your chosen AI provider → back to your computer No
Your remove.bg / Clipdrop API key Stored locally on your device only No
Plugin purchase details Handled entirely by Adobe Marketplace No

5Adobe Marketplace

If you purchase or install the Plugin through Adobe Marketplace, Adobe handles your purchase, account, and any related billing information according to its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Adobe's Privacy Policy for details on how Adobe processes that information.

6Our Website

This Privacy Policy covers the Plugin itself. Our website, artcllick.ro, does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If you choose to contact us directly by email or through a contact form on our website, we will only use the information you provide (such as your name and email address) to respond to your inquiry, and we will not share it with third parties.

7Children's Privacy

The Plugin is a professional tool intended for use by Photoshop users and businesses, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and, as noted above, the Plugin does not collect personal data from any user regardless of age.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Plugin adds support for a new third-party provider. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how the Plugin works, please contact us at Artcllickon@outlook.com.
